Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 August 2026

Short version: Leaf Peeper collects nothing, sends nothing, and has no servers. Everything the app reads stays on your device.

Information we collect

None. Leaf Peeper does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal information. There are no user accounts, no analytics or crash-reporting services, no advertising identifiers, and no third-party SDKs of any kind.

Information the app reads from your vehicle

When you connect an OBD-II adapter, the app reads diagnostic data from your car, which may include battery state of health, capacity, cell voltages, temperatures, 12V system voltage, fault codes, and the Vehicle Identification Number (VIN).

All of this is processed entirely on your device and stored only on your device. None of it is transmitted anywhere. We never see it.

The VIN is treated as personal information. It is used only on-device, to help identify your vehicle's generation and battery pack size, and it is excluded from any diagnostic log the app lets you copy or export.

Network access

Leaf Peeper does not require an internet connection and does not make network requests to any server operated by us. Communication with your OBD-II adapter happens over Bluetooth Low Energy, directly between your device and the adapter.

Data retention and deletion

Any data the app saves is stored locally on your device. Deleting the app removes it. Because we never receive your data, there is nothing for us to retain or delete on our side, and no data deletion request is necessary.

Children's privacy

Leaf Peeper is not directed at children and collects no information from anyone, including children under 13.

Third parties

If you purchased the app through the Apple App Store, Apple handles the transaction and its own privacy policy applies to that purchase. We receive only the aggregate, anonymised sales reporting Apple provides to all developers. We never receive your name, address, or payment details.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date above. Since the app collects no data, any change is likely to be clarification rather than a change in practice.
