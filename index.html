See what your Leaf's battery is really doing.

Leaf Peeper reads state of health, capacity, individual cell voltages, 12V system voltage and fault codes straight from your car, using a Bluetooth LE OBD-II adapter and your iPhone. No account, no subscription, no cloud.

What you need

  • A Nissan Leaf
  • An iPhone or iPad running iOS 17 or later
  • A Bluetooth Low Energy OBD-II adapter, such as the LELink2 BLE

Important: iOS cannot talk to classic Bluetooth adapters. If a listing says only "Bluetooth" or "ELM327" without "BLE", "Bluetooth 4.0" or "Bluetooth Low Energy", it will not work with any iPhone app, including this one. Wi-Fi adapters are not supported.

Built to be honest about what it knows

Byte layouts differ across Leaf generations. Where a reading cannot be verified for your car, Leaf Peeper says so instead of showing a confident wrong number. A bad state of health figure is worse than none at all when someone is buying a used car.

Your data stays on your phone

Leaf Peeper collects nothing. No analytics, no tracking, no servers. Everything the app reads from your car stays on your device. See the privacy policy.

Try it before you connect anything

Demo Mode is built in, so you can see every screen with realistic data before you buy an adapter or go out to the car.
