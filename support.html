Support

Email willdevsapps@gmail.com and you will get a reply from a person. Please include your Leaf's model year, your adapter make and model, and your iOS version.

My adapter will not connect

This is almost always the adapter, not the app. iOS cannot communicate with classic Bluetooth (Bluetooth 3.x) OBD-II adapters at all, and most inexpensive ELM327 dongles are exactly that. They work on Android, which is why the listings claim compatibility.

Your adapter must be Bluetooth Low Energy, described as "BLE", "Bluetooth 4.0" or "Bluetooth Low Energy". The LELink2 BLE is a known-good choice. Wi-Fi adapters are not supported.

Also note that BLE adapters are not paired in the iOS Bluetooth settings screen. Connect from inside the app instead. If you have paired it in iOS Settings, remove it there first.

The app connects but shows no battery data

Turn the car on, not just to accessory. The battery controller does not answer requests when the car is fully off. If data still does not appear, your adapter may not support the custom CAN headers this app needs. Email me with the adapter model and I will tell you whether it is on the known-good list.

A reading says "unverified" or "unsupported"

That is deliberate. Data layouts differ between Leaf generations, and the app will not show you a number it cannot stand behind. If you are seeing this on a car you think should be supported, please get in touch, since that is exactly the feedback that lets me add support for your generation.

Can I try it before buying an adapter?

Yes. Demo Mode is built into the app and available from first launch, with no adapter and no car required.

Does it work on Android?

Not yet. Leaf Peeper is iOS only for now.
